A letter from the heart

To My Dearest Sister...

Before your forever begins

To My Dearest Sister, My Dearest Friend, and My Love…

I can't exactly think of where to begin.

Whenever I think of you, my mind and heart go into the most beautiful, soft, warm and safe vibes. You are the one who, of course, knows everything about me. The one who can control me with just your eyes. Yes, I still remember… and always will.

I genuinely believe we were meant to be together.

Those black tops, yellow skirts and the same outfits at Mama's wedding… then always ending up in similar outfits. Those WOWWW, WOWWW gown moments (bohot embarrassing at the same time 😂), saath mein woh saari bakwaas baatein karna… our random plans of "Mars pe jaake baste hain" 😂… and everything.

Our bond… jisse ghar waalon ko bhi jealousy hoti hai! 😂

It's funny how we have grown up, but somewhere inside, I still see us as those two girls doing all the stupid things together, laughing over absolutely nothing and making memories out of the smallest moments. I wish I could pause some of those moments and keep them forever.

And now, I can't even imagine the phase when I didn't know that you existed. Can't even think of my life without you.

Yep, you have found your life partner, and he is definitely great. But for me… you were, are and will always be the bestest one. ❤️

I wish I could give you all the happiness, blessings, love, care, security, prosperity and everything that I have. Everything beautiful that life has to offer.

And yep, I will ALWAYS be the third wheel between you two. Not negatively, but I simply cannot leave you. You know too many of my secrets, haina? 🤔😅

Meri life ka sabse exciting event aa raha hai, jiske liye main kab se wait kar rahi thi… TERI SHADIIII!

Never thought ki itna emotional feel karungi is baare mein, but here I am… with all the happiness, a little jealousy, emotions and overwhelming feelings everywhere.

When you walk towards your new life, I hope you know that a part of me will always be standing right beside you, cheering for you, annoying you and loving you just as much as ever.

Things might change, routines might change, and we might not get to spend every moment together like before. But one thing I never want to change is us. You'll always have the same place in my life, no matter where life takes us.

Bas tu hamesha khush rahe, bakwaas karti rahe, and jo tu chahe tujhe sab mileee.

And if ever, anytime, kabhi kuch lage, kuch ho… then you know, I will ALWAYS be there for you. No matter what, no matter when.

And haan, ek baat aur…

Bata dena mere jiju ko ki tu usse pehle mere peeche baithi thi bike pe. FLEXXX! 😁😉

Bas tu ab tayyari shuru karrr!

Believe me, you were, you are AND you will ALWAYS be my love. ❤️

Our souls definitely have that BFF bonding. Some connections are just meant to be, and ours is one of them.

If I ever get another chance to choose my sister, my best friend, or my person in another lifetime, I would still choose you. Every single time.

Forever your sister,
your best friend,
your partner in all the bakwaas,
and your unofficial third wheel.

Byeeeeee! ❤️
